Agentic Anonymous

A meetup for people addicted to coding agents.

Sep 30, 2026 (tentative) 17:00 – 19:00

What this is

We're gathering people who can't stop running coding agents — whether that means building your own agent framework, running Claude Code / Codex / Cursor / whatever all day, or just generally living inside an agentic loop. Come talk shop, compare setups, and admit you have a problem.

Call for talks

We're looking for two kinds of talks:

Technical

Home‑grown coding agent frameworks, the agents you're operating day to day, tooling, workflows, war stories.

Psychological

Your addiction. How agents changed the way you think and work. We'll also touch on AI psychosis.
